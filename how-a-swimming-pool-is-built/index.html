How a Swimming Pool Is Built, Step by Step

From the first spade in the ground to the first swim: what happens at each stage of building a concrete pool, with photos from our own builds.

Get a free quote

The build at a glance

1. Design and planning

We look at your yard and agree on the size, shape, depth and position of the pool, where the steps go, and where the filter and pump will sit.

2. Excavation

The pool is marked out and dug. On tight sites without digger access, our team digs by hand.

3. Plumbing

The pipes for the pump, filter, inlets and lights are laid before the concrete goes in.

4. Steel and concrete

Steel is fixed in the hole and the concrete shell is cast and plastered. This is what makes the pool strong.

5. Tiling and coping

Coping stones finish the edge and waterline tiles go on. Steps get their tiles too.

6. Finishing

The inside gets its marbelite or fibreglass finish, and the filter, pump and any lights or water features are connected.

7. Completed

The pool is filled, cleaned and handed over, ready to swim.

Questions

How long does it take to build a swimming pool?

Do I need plans for a pool?

Most municipalities need building plans for a new pool, and pools must be fenced or covered for child safety. Check with your municipality before building. [CCM to confirm whether they help with plans.]

What size pool should I build?

A 5m x 4m pool suits most family gardens. Smaller plunge pools fit tight yards; long narrow pools fit down the side of a house. Send us the space and we'll suggest a size.

Marbelite or fibreglass finish?

Marbelite is the classic plaster finish. Fibreglass is smoother and easier to clean. Both are done by our own team.
